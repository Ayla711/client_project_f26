Explore Internship Opportunities

Connect Your Learning with Professional Experience

An internship gives you an opportunity to apply what you have learned, develop new skills, and explore your career interests. Use this guide to consider possible paths, find opportunities, and prepare for your search with support from the UMSI Career Development Office (CDO).

Explore Internship Paths

The 2025 BSI Internship Report includes these examples of past student placements. Use them as starting points when exploring current listings and deciding which roles interest you.

Data and Analytics

Past placements include a data analytics internship at Ally Financial. Consider this area if you want to explore how your interests in data connect with professional work.

Design and User Experience

Students have held experience design internships at Adobe and UX design internships at Costco Wholesale. Explore these kinds of roles as you consider where to apply your design interests.

Product and Business

Past examples include a product internship at 365 Retail Markets and a business analyst internship at Capital One. Review similar listings to learn which skills and experiences employers seek.

Find Opportunities and Meet Employers

Start with UMSI CareerLink

CareerLink brings together internship listings, employer profiles, career support, and events. Search for roles that interest you, explore organizations, and set notifications for opportunities in your preferred industries.

Access is available to enrolled UMSI students and alumni. Incoming students receive access when their first term begins.

Log in to UMSI CareerLink or learn about CareerLink.

Attend the UMSI Career Fair

The UMSI Career Fair gives you an opportunity to meet employers, learn about their organizations, and discuss internship and job opportunities. Use these conversations to explore roles and ask which skills employers are looking for.

  • Research employers: Review participating organizations and their available positions. Identify the employers you want to meet and prepare questions about their work.
  • Prepare your resume: Update your resume and bring printed copies to an in-person fair. Check the event instructions for opportunities to share it through a career fair resume book.
  • Look for preparation sessions: Check for CDO sessions that can help you get ready for the fair.

Read the preparation tips from the 2026 UMSI Career Fair. For upcoming dates and registration details, check UMSI career events and CareerLink.

Learn Through Employer Connections

The CDO offers several ways to learn about organizations and connect with people across industries:

  • Explore campus recruiting: Look for employer information sessions, interviews, and office hours.
  • Learn about different workplaces: Explore Career Insider employer visits and industry panels.

Find these employer engagement options on the UMSI Career Resources page and browse upcoming events in CareerLink.

Prepare for Your Internship Search

Take time to identify what you want to learn and how your experience relates to the roles you are considering.

Search Preparation Goals

  • Clarify your interests: Consider the skills you want to develop and the kinds of work you want to explore.
  • Review your materials: Connect your coursework and project experience to your resume, cover letter, and portfolio.
  • Practice your conversations: Prepare to explain your work in interviews and when meeting employers.

Use Career Resources and Feedback

Current students can use the Career Resource Library in Canvas for guidance on applications, networking, and interviewing. The CDO also offers mock interviews and resume and portfolio reviews.

Career coaching gives you time to discuss your questions, review application materials, and practice interviewing. Peer coaches can also share support informed by their own internship searches.

Plan Your Next Steps

  1. Explore listings: Review responsibilities and qualifications for roles that interest you.
  2. Prepare an application: Select examples from your experience that relate to the position.
  3. Ask for feedback: Use career resources or meet with a coach to discuss your approach.
  4. Stay organized: Keep track of application deadlines, employer conversations, and next steps.

To understand how internships fit into your degree, review the UMSI internship guidance. It explains program options, internship credit, and grants that may support eligible unpaid or underpaid experiences.
